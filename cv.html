Research Experience

CBA Player Influence Model Ongoing

Independent research project

  • Built a reproducible possession-level pipeline using official CBA records and K8 play-by-play data across 420 regular-season and 33 playoff games from the 2025-26 season.
  • Reconstructed five-player lineups, audited substitution and clock anomalies, and generated a standardized event and possession dataset.
  • Estimated ridge RAPM, hierarchical empirical-Bayes RAPM, exact-lineup interactions, and variance-component Laplace approximations.
  • Evaluated ranking stability, playoff prediction, simulation recovery, and interval calibration. Game-bootstrap mean Spearman correlation was 0.786 for offense and 0.762 for defense.
  • Found that first-ridge nominal 95% intervals understate uncertainty, with calibration factors up to 2.999 at strong simulated signal.
