CBA Player Influence Model CBA 球员影响力模型

Built a reproducible player-influence pipeline from official CBA and K8 play-by-play data, covering 420 regular-season and 33 playoff games in the 2025–26 season.

Reconstructed five-player lineups and possessions, then compared ridge RAPM, hierarchical empirical-Bayes RAPM, lineup-interaction models, and simulation-based uncertainty calibration.

Role: Data pipeline, statistical modeling, and validation

Coverage: 2025–26 season · Ongoing

基于官方 CBA 数据与 K8 回合事件构建可复现的球员影响力分析流程，覆盖 2025–26 赛季 420 场常规赛和 33 场季后赛。

完成五人阵容与回合重建，并比较 Ridge RAPM、经验贝叶斯层级 RAPM、阵容交互模型及基于模拟的不确定性校准。

职责：数据流程、统计建模与结果验证

数据范围：2025–26 赛季 · 持续进行
