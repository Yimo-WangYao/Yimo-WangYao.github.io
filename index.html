Featured Research

CBA Player Influence Model

核心研究

CBA 球员影响力模型

Research question: How reliably can individual CBA player influence be estimated when player exposure is sparse, lineups are highly collinear, and possession outcomes are noisy?

I built a reproducible possession-level pipeline that integrates official CBA records with K8 play-by-play data, reconstructs five-player lineups, and estimates adjusted player effects.

453Regular-season and playoff games
287Players meeting the exposure floor
200Game-level bootstrap resamples

Methods: Ridge RAPM, hierarchical empirical-Bayes RAPM, exact-lineup interaction checks, variance-component Laplace approximation, controlled simulation, and interval calibration.

Key finding: CBA rankings remain unstable under weak lineup signal. Mean bootstrap rank correlation was 0.786 for offense and 0.762 for defense, and nominal 95% intervals required calibration factors up to 2.999.

Limitations: The estimates are adjusted on-court associations, not causal effects. The current model does not fully identify hidden lineup interactions or unobserved player roles.

研究问题：当球员样本有限、阵容高度共线且回合结果噪声较大时，CBA 球员个人影响力能够被多可靠地估计？

我构建了以回合为单位的可复现分析流程，整合官方 CBA 记录与 K8 回合事件，重建五人阵容并估计调整后的球员效应。

453常规赛与季后赛比赛
287达到样本门槛的球员
200比赛级 Bootstrap 重采样

方法：Ridge RAPM、经验贝叶斯层级 RAPM、精确阵容交互检验、方差组件 Laplace 近似、控制模拟与区间校准。

核心发现：弱阵容信号下，CBA 球员排名仍不稳定。Bootstrap 排名平均相关系数为进攻 0.786、防守 0.762；名义 95% 区间的校准系数最高达到 2.999。

局限：当前估计是控制场上因素后的关联，而非因果效应；模型尚未完全识别隐藏的阵容交互与不可观测球员角色。
